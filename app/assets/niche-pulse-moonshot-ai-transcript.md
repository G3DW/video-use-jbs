When the machine can synthesize data, write production code, and out-analyze a human for fractions of a penny, the inherent value of raw intelligence drops to zero. What takes its place?

Will the new bottleneck be heavily guarded, original human data that hasn't been polluted by synthetic AI feedback loops? Will the ultimate premium be placed on physical, real-world robotics that can actually manifest that cheap intelligence into tactile physical labor? Or perhaps, will the ultimate scarce resource simply be human taste? The distinct creative vision, the empathy, the lived experience, and the unique editorial perspective of the human operator who is actually writing the constraints and directing the machine.

Because if every single business on the planet has instantaneous free access to the exact same genius-level digital intern, the only thing that differentiates your product from the noise is the specific quality of the vision you mandate. Your taste becomes your ultimate moat. Keep questioning the architecture, keep building your modular furniture, and keep refining your taste.
